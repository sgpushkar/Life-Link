# Project Overview

## Problem Statement

Healthcare facilities in rural and semi-urban regions often work in information silos. Oxygen cylinders, blood units, ICU equipment, and transport capacity are tracked manually or via fragmented systems. This creates delays during emergencies and results in avoidable waste, shortages, and mismatches between supply and demand.

Common challenges include:

- emergency stockouts going unnoticed,
- equipment or blood being underutilized at nearby facilities,
- reliance on manual calls, informal coordination, and delayed updates,
- inability to prioritize high-urgency medical need over low-urgency requests,
- poor visibility for district authorities and transport teams.

## Solution

LifeLink acts as a real-time healthcare resource coordination network. It connects facilities, blood banks, donors, and logistics teams on a single platform, giving each stakeholder a live view of critical resources and helping redistribute them where they are most urgently needed.

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Live inventory visibility
Facilities can track oxygen, ventilators, ICU items, and blood stock in one place with up-to-date status information across multiple locations.

### 2. Urgency-based prioritization
Requests are ranked by urgency, time sensitivity, patient criticality, and distance. This allows the system to push critical needs ahead of routine or low-priority cases.

### 3. Blood compatibility and expiry handling
The solution supports blood inventory tracking, compatibility checks, and near-expiry redistribution suggestions to reduce avoidable wastage.

### 4. Logistics coordination
Transport jobs, pickups, handovers, and delivery updates are tracked in real time to support safe movement of high-value resources.

### 5. Low-bandwidth resilience
The platform is designed to support rural and low-connectivity environments using simplified interfaces, SMS-style interactions, and offline-first patterns.

### 6. District oversight
Health administrators can review trends, gaps, and utilization patterns to make better operational decisions.

## Target Users

- Primary Health Centres and Community Health Centres
- District hospitals and blood banks
- Emergency transport drivers
- Donors and public health volunteers
- District health authorities and administrators

## Impact

LifeLink is designed to improve:

- response time during critical shortages,
- utilization of nearby surplus inventory,
- blood safety and waste reduction,
- administrative visibility across the district,
- readiness for low-connectivity healthcare environments.

## Innovation Highlights

- real-time resource matching and prioritization,
- transparent clinical and logistics scoring logic,
- interoperability-friendly healthcare coordination model,
- rapid triage of emergency requirements,
- usability for both digital and low-bandwidth contexts.

## Business / Social Value

This platform supports more equitable and resilient healthcare operations by reducing delays and improving the flow of critical resources where they are most needed. It aligns with the broader goal of strengthening emergency preparedness, district coordination, and patient outcomes in underserved regions.
