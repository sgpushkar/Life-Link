## Project at a Glance

- Project name: LifeLink
- Category: HealthTech / Public Health Infrastructure / Rural Emergency Logistics
- Core idea: Real-time coordination of critical medical equipment and blood inventory across facilities in a district.
- Primary objective: reduce stockouts, prevent wastage, and match urgent patient needs with nearby surplus resources in near real time.

## Why This Matters

Rural health systems often suffer from fragmented inventory visibility, delayed communication, and manual coordination across hospitals, blood banks, and transport teams. LifeLink creates a unified operational layer that helps facilities identify shortages, prioritize critical patients, and redistribute urgently needed equipment and blood units faster and more transparently.

## Submission Summary

LifeLink is an integrated healthcare logistics platform that combines:

- live inventory tracking for oxygen, ventilators, ICU beds, and blood resources,
- urgency-based request prioritization,
- donor and blood bank management,
- logistics and handover workflows,
- low-bandwidth / SMS-enabled fallback for rural environments,
- district oversight dashboards for health administrators.

This project is intentionally designed to be practical, deployable, and understandable in a demo setting while also reflecting real operational needs in Indian healthcare delivery environments.
