# Judge Demo Script

## Objective

Show the audience how LifeLink transforms fragmented, slow healthcare coordination into a real-time emergency response workflow.

## Demo Flow

### 1. Open the landing experience
Begin with the main dashboard or landing page to show the district-level healthcare network, map, and inventory visibility.

### 2. Display the shortage scenario
Switch to a rural facility persona with an active shortage of critical medical resources. Show the inventory view and current shortage status.

### 3. Create an urgent request
Demonstrate how a facility creates a resource request with urgency, quantity, and time sensitivity. Highlight the automated logic and request scoring.

### 4. Show matching and prioritization
Explain how the system ranks requests based on criticality, urgency, distance, scarcity, and waiting time. This is the core innovation of the platform.

### 5. Match to nearest viable surplus
Show how nearby facilities with surplus equipment or blood are identified and surfaced to the requesting team.

### 6. Accept and allocate the resource
Demonstrate the accept flow and show how the system locks the allocation and creates a logistics task or handover pathway.

### 7. Show live logistics updates
Switch to the transport or logistics view and show the job state progressing through pickup, transit, and delivery stages.

### 8. Highlight blood and donor management
Open a blood bank or donor flow to show shelf-life handling, compatibility data, and community donor engagement.

### 9. Demonstrate low-bandwidth readiness
Show how the system can operate in reduced connectivity scenarios or simplified SMS-style interactions, emphasizing usability for rural environments.

## Key Talking Points

- We reduce hours of manual coordination to a few minutes.
- We prioritize life-critical needs instead of first-in-first-out ordering.
- We help underused nearby resources reach urgent demand quickly.
- We support both digital and low-bandwidth health workflows.

## Suggested Closing Statement

LifeLink does not just track inventory; it creates a coordination layer for emergency response. It helps healthcare teams act faster, prioritize the right patients, and reduce waste while preserving accountability across the entire chain of care.
